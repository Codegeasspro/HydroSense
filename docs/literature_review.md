# Literature review matrix

This matrix records only references whose bibliographic metadata have been checked. It is a planning aid, not evidence that a cited method has been reproduced or validated in HydroSense.

| Reference | System and data | Method | HydroSense use | Limitation when transferred to HydroSense |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ruiz-Diaz et al. (2026) | Systematic review of 91 publications on water-distribution-network leak detection | D-A-T taxonomy | Classify sensing, processing architecture, and diagnostic task; frame validation gap | Review does not supply a single-reservoir protocol |
| Masoudimoghaddam et al. (2025) | Low-cost ultrasonic device evaluated in laboratory and field channel conditions | Device-specific calibration and error analysis | Define ultrasonic calibration, repeatability, and reporting practice | Reported error does not transfer automatically to an HC-SR04 or reservoir geometry |
| Ghorbani Bam et al. (2025) | Review of 147 water-sector digital-twin studies | Digital-twin component analysis | Define synchronization, model update, uncertainty, and decision requirements | Review does not validate HydroSense hardware or control logic |
| Steins et al. (2026) | Real-world-network pressure data with synthetic leaks | Adaptive nonparametric CUSUM | Benchmark a statistical detector against physical residual logic | Pressure-network detector must be reformulated for storage-volume data |

The candidate literature list will be expanded only after full-text review confirms the stated method, dataset, and performance claims.
